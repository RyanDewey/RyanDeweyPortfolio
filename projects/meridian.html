Meridian

Geographic Market Intelligence Platform

Overview

Meridian is a data pipeline and visualization tool that combines demographic and business datasets to map market demand geographically. Given a target industry or business type, it surfaces where demand is high and supply is low — identifying underserved markets at a glance.

The project was born from a practical question: where should a new business open? Meridian answers that by pulling structured data into PostgreSQL, running queries to score geographic areas, and rendering the results as an interactive map.

Technical Approach

Data Pipeline

Raw demographic and business data is ingested, cleaned, and loaded into a PostgreSQL database using a Python ETL pipeline. SQL queries join and aggregate the datasets by geographic region, computing demand-to-supply ratios that score each area's market potential.

Geographic Visualization

Scored regions are rendered onto an interactive map using Python's data visualization stack. Color-coded overlays make high-opportunity zones immediately readable, letting users quickly identify and compare markets without digging into raw numbers.

Tech Stack

Python SQL PostgreSQL Pandas Data Visualization ETL
